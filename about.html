About Our Platform

Primary School English Teacher Resources

Who We Are

Welcome to Primary School English Teacher Resources, a platform designed to support English teachers in Algerian primary schools. Our goal is to make teaching resources easier to access and use.

What We Offer

The platform provides organised flashcards for the English sequences taught in the third, fourth, and fifth years of primary school.

Resources are arranged by school year and sequence, helping teachers find the flashcards they need without spending unnecessary time searching for them across different sources.

How Teachers Can Use the Flashcards

Teachers can display the flashcards using a classroom projector or print them for classroom activities. Many flashcards also include listening buttons to help teachers present vocabulary.

Our Aim

Our aim is to help teachers save preparation time by providing an organised and accessible collection of flashcards in one place. We hope this platform will make preparing English lessons easier and more convenient.
